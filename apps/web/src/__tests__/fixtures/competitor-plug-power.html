Overview

Scalable PEM electrolyzer solutions for clean hydrogen production.

Plug's PEM electrolyzer product family delivers reliable, scalable hydrogen generation solutions designed to support a wide range of industrial and energy applications. Built on decades of proven proton exchange membrane (PEM) technology experience, Plug systems are engineered for high performance, operational flexibility, and seamless integration with renewable power sources.

Standardized to large-scale

From standardized containerized systems to large-scale integrated deployments, Plug offers electrolyzer solutions designed to meet varying production capacities, site requirements, and project timelines.
