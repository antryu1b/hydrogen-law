HyLink®
Alkaline 23

Sunfire sets a new benchmark in large-scale hydrogen production with HyLink® Alkaline 23. This outdoor, 30 bar(g) pressurized alkaline electrolysis system of generation 3 is based on Sunfire’s industry-proven generation 2 stack technology. The system is modular, preassembled, and delivers up to 1,000 kg/h of green hydrogen. HyLink® Alkaline 23 provides the fundamentals required to execute bankable green hydrogen projects successfully: The lowest total plant CapEx, faster and simpler construction and commissioning, high hydrogen production efficiency, and operational flexibility with stable long-term performance.
