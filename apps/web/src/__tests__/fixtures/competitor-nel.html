Spec Sheet

Nel's MC500 PEM electrolyser has everything you need, and nothing you don't.

≤10% per second ramp rate

Cold start in under 8 minutes; supports automatic load following for variable power sources.

Fully containerized PEM

Factory acceptance tested (FAT), 20 ft + 40 ft outdoor enclosures, simple transportation and installation.

99.9995% purity

Meets ISO 14687:2019 & SAE J2719 with integrated high-purity dryer package.

No deoxo required

Full differential pressure eliminates oxygen removal systems, reducing cost and complexity.

Integrated RO/EDI system

Uses tap water and purifies it internally, no external water treatment needed.
